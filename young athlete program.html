Young Athlete Program

Aims at empowering young people, educators, coaches and families as leaders for inclusion, starting their local schools and communities around the world.
This work is done through Youth Innovation Projects, Global Unified Schools, Global Center for Inclusion, Youth Leaderships, Young Athletes and Spread the word: Inclusion etcetera.

Special Olympics Young Athletes is a sport and play program designed for children with and without intellectual disabilities, ages 2 to 7.
It's all about introducing these young children to the world of sports in a fun, inclusive, and engaging way.



Young Athletes is a fantastic program that makes a real difference in the lives of young children.
It's about creating a world where everyone can play, learn, and grow together.
