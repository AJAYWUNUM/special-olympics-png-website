Connect

Connect within Special Olympics Papua New Guinea stiers a rising pattern where we and our athletes bond. But not just bonding is part of Connect, we collaborate with our athletes to enhance their social and internet skills as well.
In essence, "connect" in Special Olympics signifies the organization's commitment to building bridges, fostering relationships, and creating a supportive network where athletes with intellectual disabilities can thrive, both on and off the field.

Collaborate

Collaboration within Special Olympics Papua New Guinea fosters inclusion, empowerment, and positive impact to craete an inclusive environmrnt. If you have more questions or need further information, please do no hasitate to contact us. Our team will be very happy to talk with you.
Collaboration is essential to the success of Special Olympics. By working together, these various stakeholders create a supportive and inclusive environment where athletes with intellectual disabilities can thrive and achieve their full potential.

Grow

In order to grow SOPNG reaches out to school to inform them of what we as an organisation do. We are agents of change, for creating a inclusive society.
Special Olympics not only provides physical benefits but also helps individuals with intellectual disabilities develop confidence, social skills, and a sense of belonging. It fosters a supportive community where athletes, families, volunteers, and supporters come together to celebrate achievements and promote inclusion.
