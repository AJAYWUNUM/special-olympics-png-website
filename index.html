WHAT WE DO

Ability in action.
Belonging for all.

Special Olympics Papua New Guinea creates year-round opportunities for people with intellectual disabilities to take part in sport, build skills and grow as leaders. Athletes are supported by families, coaches, volunteers, health partners and young people working together to make inclusion visible in everyday community life.

OUR STORY

Inclusion grows
when we show up.

SOPNG brings athletes, families, volunteers, coaches and community partners together around a shared belief: everyone should have a chance to participate. Through local activities, school visits, health initiatives and leadership opportunities, people can learn from one another and help make communities more welcoming.
