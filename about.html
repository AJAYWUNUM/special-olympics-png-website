OUR JOURNEY

Rooted in PNG.
Part of a global movement.

2012

SOPNG is established

Special Olympics Papua New Guinea began as a Founding Committee Member Group on 27 September 2012.

2015

Accredited program

SOPNG became a fully accredited program affiliated with Special Olympics International and Special Olympics Asia Pacific.

Today

Growing inclusion locally

Sport, health, family support and youth leadership bring athletes and communities together.
