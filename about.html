About Us

It’s at the local level—right here—where interested volunteers meet the athletes. That’s where the perceptions start to change and where the miracle of transformation takes place.

Aim

To provide year-round sports training and athletic competition in a variety of Olympic-type sports for children and adults with an intellectual disability, giving them continuing opportunities to develop physical fitness, demonstrate courage, experience joy and participate in the sharing of gifts, skills and friendship with their families, other special Olympic athletes and the community.

Special Olympics Papua New Guinea

Special Olympics Papua New Guinea was established on the 27th of September 2012 as a Founding Committee Member Group.
In 2015, SOPNG was recognized as a fully accredited program affiliated with Special Olympics International (SOI) and Special Olympics Asia Pacific (SOAP). SOPNG is also incorporated with the Investment Promotion Authority (IPA).

Special Olympics

Special Olympics is a global movement that unleashes the human spirit through the transformative power and joy of sports, every day around the world. We are dedicated to empowering athletes with intellectual disabilities to become physically fit, productive, and respected members of society through sports training and competition.

FOUNDING COMMITTEE MEMBERS

The Founding Committee Members of SOPNG include, Olympian Takale Tuna (President), Paralympian Ben Theodore -V/President, Cutie Frances Moi (Treasurer), Sophia Tuna (/Secretary/Sports Program Manager), Lady Janet ToRobert (Special Projects), Bennie Moi (Family Program), Thomas Kahai (Youth Program), Dr Gideon Kendino (Healthy Athletes Program), William & Helen Nege (Parents rep), Thompson Ninkama (Athletes rep), and Saimona Mokupe (Physiotherapist).

Mission

To provide year-round sports training and athletic competition in a variety of Olympic-type sports for children and adults with intellectual disabilities, giving them continued opportunities to develop physical fitness, demonstrate courage, experience joy, and participate in a sharing of gifts, skills, and friendship with their families, other Special Olympics athletes, and the community.

Athlete Oath

“Let me win. But if I cannot win, let me be brave in the attempt.”

Scope

  • To establish a comprehensive sporting ability program for children and adults with intellectual disabilities and their different abilities.
  • To support exciting programs and institutions dedicated to inclusive training.
  • To establish an enabling environment for the wider community to ensure equal opportunities are provided for people with intellectual disabilities in PNG.

History

Founded on the 27th of September 2012, Special Olympics Papua New Guinea (SOPNG) was established to provide sports training and athletic competition for individuals with intellectual disabilities.

In 2015, SOPNG was recognized as a fully accredited program affiliated with Special Olympics International (SOI) and Special Olympics Asia Pacific (SOAP). SOPNG is also incorporated with the Investment Promotion Authority (IPA).

PROGRAMS

Special Olympics PNG has had a full schedule of events for the last 5 years and this committee works really hard to raise funds to implement all its programs. All committee members are volunteers who take time from their busy work commitments to run SOPNG. So far, there are programs in the Nations Capital, Central Province and the Autonomous Region of Bougainville and East New Britain. A sub-program was launched in Bougainville in May 2013 call Special Olympics Bougainville (SOB) which is run by Mark Ketsin (Chairman), Victor Lomon (V/chairman) Grace Kehono (Secretary). A sub-Program in East New Britain called Special Olympics East New Britain which is run by Bennie Moi (Chairman) and his executives. Special Olympics PNG is currently running three (5) programs, Sports, Healthy Communities, Family Network Support, Young Athletes and Youth Activation.
